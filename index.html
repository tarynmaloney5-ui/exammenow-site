How it works

From topic to class results in four steps.

One box to start, a quick check, and results you can act on the same day.

  1. WriteType a topic, pick 5 to 20 questions and a level, and paste your own notes if you like.
  2. CheckRead every question and fix anything. AI can make mistakes, so nothing is shared until you approve it.
  3. ShareSend the link or show the QR code. Students open it in a browser and type their first name.
  4. See resultsScores for each student and the questions most of the class missed, so you know what to reteach.

Privacy

No student accounts. Almost no student data.

Students type only a first name. ExamMe saves that name and which questions they got right, and deletes it after 90 days. Your topic and notes go to an AI service to write the questions. Read the full privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I use ExamMe without installing it?

Yes. Open ExamMe in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. It's the same app as on Android. Your study history is saved in that browser, so it won't follow you to another device.

When can I download ExamMe?

The Android app is in early testing on Google Play. Become a tester to install it now. The iPhone app is in testing through TestFlight, Apple's testing app: join from the tester page. A public release will follow once testing is done.

How do I become a tester?

Send us the Gmail address you use on Google Play. We'll add you to the test and email you a link to install ExamMe. See Become a tester for the steps.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. iPhone testing is open through Apple's TestFlight app; the join link is on the tester page. It isn't on the App Store yet. Meanwhile, you can use ExamMe in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.

Who is ExamMe for?

Teachers, tutors and anyone running a class. Students of any age take your exams in a web browser with just a first name. No student accounts or app needed.

Is the AI always right?

No. Questions are written by AI and can contain mistakes, which is why you check and edit every question before students see it. Pasting your own notes keeps questions to the facts you teach.

Do students need the app?

No. Students open your link or scan the QR code on any phone, tablet or computer, type their first name and hand in. Their answers are marked straight away.

Is there a limit on exams?

You can share 3 exams free. A one-time $2.99 purchase in the app unlocks unlimited shared exams. The AI writes up to 5 new exams a day per phone. Students taking your exams never count toward any limit.

Do I need an account?

No. Teachers and students don't sign up. Your exams and their private results links live on your phone, so keep the app installed to see results.
